How this was done

1,371 profiles were read in full and 69 from unrelated sectors were read as a check. Each got a verdict and a reason: 27 scored here, 7 not attending, 240 held, 1,166 dropped.

The own website of every advancing company and 58 held ones was read. Fourteen were promoted and twelve downgraded where the site did not support the Slush profile.

Funding, founders and customers for the leaders were checked against press, investor pages, registers and LinkedIn.

Live Slush profiles can differ from the exported data. One ranking changed for that reason, so check the live profile before sending a request.

Scores are a judgement on a fixed rubric, not a measurement. Three sites could not be read: Gnoses, Krida and SDR Prestige.

Working notes for one week of meetings. Not an endorsement of any company.
